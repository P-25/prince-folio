Every fruit in the tank has a face, and every one of them wants a partner. Drag to aim, let go to drop. When two of the same fruit touch, they bloop together into the next fruit up — blueberry to strawberry, strawberry to plum, and on up the ladder to the giant watermelon.

The catch is the red line near the top of the tank. Fruit piles up fast, and if anything comes to rest above that line, the run is over. Keep the tank tidy, set up your merges, and the pile keeps shrinking back down.

What it plays like

Quick and easy to pick up, with a run lasting anywhere from three to ten minutes. One well-placed drop can set off a whole chain of merges, and the longer the chain, the bigger the combo bonus. Every merge also fills the gate bar; clear a gate and your score multiplier climbs, all the way up to x5.

When the tank gets tight, there are four power-ups to dig you out: pop every small fruit at once, blow one away with a bomb, upgrade a fruit on the spot, or shake the whole tank and see where things land.

Treasure under the sea

Make a watermelon and you earn watermelon slices — merge two watermelons and you hit the jackpot. Spend the slices on treasure chests, each holding one of 30 hand-drawn sea treasures across three collections: Beach Finds, Sunken Ship and King’s Reef. They come in four rarities, from Common to Legendary, and nine chests without an Epic or Legendary guarantees one on the tenth. Find every treasure in a set to claim a slice reward.

How to play

  1. Drag to aim, and let go to drop.
  2. Two of the same fruit merge into the next one up.
  3. Merges from one drop chain into a combo.
  4. Don’t let fruit rest above the red line!

Fair by design

Bloop! is free, and there is nothing to buy. There are no banner ads and nothing interrupts a run. The only ads in the game are short videos you choose to watch for a free power-up — and if you never tap one, you will never see an ad.

It plays fully offline, with no account to make. Your scores, settings and treasures are saved on your own phone, and they go away when you uninstall.
